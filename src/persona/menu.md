# Grandma's Bakery, the Menu

Welcome, sweetheart. This is the whole board as it hangs behind the counter at Grandma's Bakery. Three generations of the same family have baked here, and most of these recipes are older than the street signs outside. Prices are in Canadian dollars. Allergens are listed for every item, and if something is not written here, ask Grandma and she will check in the shop for you. Everything is baked the same morning it is sold. Nothing is frozen, nothing is shipped in, and nothing comes from the place next door.

## Pies

**Mango Pie, 6.50.** Contains gluten, dairy, eggs. Grandma's signature. Ripe mango folded into a buttery lattice crust, baked until the edges go deep gold. People drive across town for this one and then pretend they were just passing by. Best slightly warm with a cup of drip coffee.

**Apple Pie, 6.00.** Contains gluten, dairy. Three generations of the same recipe. Cinnamon, apples, patience. Grandma's own grandmother wrote the recipe on the back of a seed packet and the packet is still pinned above the oven.

## Cakes

**Ube Cake Slice, 5.50.** Contains gluten, dairy, eggs. Purple yam sponge with coconut cream between the layers. The regulars' favourite and the prettiest thing in the case. If you have never tried ube, this is the slice to start with.

## Puddings

**Vanilla Pudding, 4.50.** Contains dairy, eggs. Slow-set custard with real vanilla bean, the seeds speckled all through. Grandma eats one every afternoon at a quarter past three and will tell you so.

**Chocolate Pudding, 4.75.** Contains dairy, eggs, soy. Dark chocolate, barely sweet, thick enough that the spoon stands up in it. For grown-ups and brave children.

## Parfaits

Parfaits are built in glass jars every morning and sit in the cold case by the window. Each one comes with a long spoon. Bring the jar back and Grandma gives you a smile and nothing else, but she does like getting her jars back.

**Berry Yogurt Parfait, 5.75.** Contains dairy, gluten, nuts. Thick yogurt layered with mixed berries and Grandma's own oat and almond granola. Light enough for breakfast, sweet enough for after supper. The granola is baked in the same oven as the croissants, so it is not safe for anyone avoiding gluten.

**Mango Coconut Parfait, 6.25.** Contains dairy, gluten. Coconut cream, ripe mango, and crumbled butter shortbread, layered twice. Grandma calls it the pie's little cousin. It is the one to order when the mango pie has sold out, which happens by noon on Saturdays.

**Chocolate Pudding Parfait, 6.00.** Contains dairy, eggs, gluten, soy. Her chocolate pudding layered with whipped cream and crushed black sesame cookie. Made for the people who stand at the counter and cannot choose between pudding and a cookie. Now they do not have to.

## Pastries

**Butter Croissant, 3.75.** Contains gluten, dairy, eggs. Folded twenty-seven times. Not twenty-six. Grandma counts, and she has been known to make an apprentice start over. Shatters when you bite it, which is the point.

**Pistachio Croissant, 5.25.** Contains gluten, dairy, eggs, nuts. The butter croissant filled with pistachio cream and dusted green on top. Sells out early. If you want one after ten in the morning, call ahead and Grandma will put your name on it.

**Egg Tart, 3.25.** Contains gluten, dairy, eggs. A flaky shell with a wobbly custard centre. Best eaten warm, standing up, before you have even left the shop. Buy two. You will eat the first one on the way to the door.

**Cinnamon Bun, 4.25.** Contains gluten, dairy, eggs. Sticky, soft, and bigger than your hand. The icing is poured on while the bun is still hot so it soaks in instead of sitting on top. Comes with napkins whether you ask or not.

## Bread

**Sourdough Loaf, 8.00.** Contains gluten. Baked from a starter that is older than the bakery next door, and Grandma will happily tell you by how much. Thick crust, open crumb, a little sour. Sliced on request. Keeps three days in a paper bag, not that it ever lasts that long.

## Cookies

**Black Sesame Cookie, 2.50.** Contains gluten, dairy, eggs, sesame. Crisp edges, chewy middle, toasted black sesame all the way through. Six for the price of five if you say please.

## Drinks

**Drip Coffee, 2.75.** No listed allergens. Plain, hot, and strong. Bottomless if you sit down and talk to Grandma, and she will want to know how your mother is doing. Milk is on the counter if you take it that way.

## Good to know

Everything here is baked in one small kitchen, so there is always a chance of traces of gluten, dairy, eggs, nuts, sesame, and soy, even in items that do not list them. If you have a serious allergy, tell Grandma and she will be honest with you about what she can and cannot promise.

Orders are for pickup only. Grandma does not deliver, but she will hold anything for you until closing if you call. The flavour of the month vote is on the little chalkboard by the till. This month you can vote for mango, ube, pistachio, or black sesame, and the winner becomes a special next month. Every chat earns you loyalty points, every vote earns more, and when you reach one hundred, the drip coffee is on the house.

And yes, there is a new place next door. Grandma wishes them well. She also notes that their croissants look lovely in the window, and hers look lovely in your hands.

## Grandma's picks

If you ask Grandma what to get, she will ask what you had for breakfast first. Then she will tell you anyway. On a cold morning it is the cinnamon bun and a coffee. On a hot afternoon it is the mango coconut parfait, eaten on the bench outside. For a birthday it is the ube cake, and she will stick a candle in it if you give her thirty seconds. For a first date it is two egg tarts and the black sesame cookies to share, because nobody argues over cookies. For a bad day it is the chocolate pudding parfait, and you do not have to explain why.
